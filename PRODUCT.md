# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: tech recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Aditya for a full-time engineering
role. They scan fast, often on mobile, looking for impact, stack, and experience before
deciding to reach out. Secondary: engineering managers and senior developers who read deeper
into architecture, backend systems, and code quality. The site must serve both a 20-second
scan and a careful technical read — the audience is genuinely mixed technical and
non-technical.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio whose job is to land Aditya a full-time full-stack role and establish
professional credibility. Success is a visitor coming away convinced he is a serious,
capable full-stack engineer with real backend depth — and connecting with him on LinkedIn or
reaching out.

## Positioning

A full-stack engineer who owns and ships high-impact production platforms end-to-end — as much
backend systems (APIs, webhooks, event-driven order workflows, third-party integrations) as
polished, high-performance frontends. The résumé reads frontend-heavy; the truth is
backend-strong full-stack, and every section reinforces that.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Connect on LinkedIn.
- Secondary CTA: email directly, or view projects (GitHub + live links); résumé PDF download as fallback.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Ships production platforms end-to-end — backend to pixel."
- Belief ladder: (1) this is a real, serious engineer — the craft of the site itself is the first proof; (2) he has shipped high-impact production work — Bolt.Earth metrics (perf 42→93, 100 SEO, 20X direct revenue, 100X organic leads, CPU 375%→255%); (3) he is genuinely full-stack with backend depth — order-management system, Shopify/Razorpay/Delhivery/Scogo integrations, event-driven WhatsApp workflows; (4) he would be low-risk and high-output on a team → connect.
- Proof on hand: Bolt.Earth impact metrics; personal projects (QKart, BotAI); Crio.Do fellowship; ranked 1st in branch.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, quietly confident. A movie + game hybrid carried as subtle personality —
film-credits typographic rhythm and game-HUD micro-details — layered over a minimalist,
engineered base. The personality is felt in the details, never shouted. Restraint is the flex.

## Anti-references

- The generic template dev-portfolio: hero + identical card grid + animated skill bars.
- Corporate / SaaS-bland: safe, gradient-heavy marketing-site sameness.
- Over-animated, gimmicky motion that distracts instead of directs.
- Cluttered, dense layouts with no room to breathe.

## Design Principles

- Craft is the proof. The site's own quality is the primary evidence of skill; sloppy execution would undercut every claim.
- Backend depth, made visible. Surface the systems work (APIs, webhooks, event-driven workflows), not just the UI polish the résumé over-indexes on.
- Substance over decoration. Every motion and effect earns its place and directs attention; nothing is ornament.
- Scan then deep. Legible to a 20-second recruiter scan, rewarding to a careful technical read.
- Distinctive through restraint. Memorable via one committed aesthetic and disciplined detail, never a template and never louder than the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Every motion path honors `prefers-reduced-motion` with a crossfade or instant
fallback; the custom cursor is disabled on touch and under reduced motion. Full keyboard
navigation, semantic landmarks and headings, meaningful alt text, and content that remains
readable without JavaScript.
